CHAPTER III · INVENTIONS & APPARATUS

The Apparatus: Featured Works

FIG. 3.1 ★ 1st Prize Winner · ₹10,000 Cash Award

RaceXplorer — Optical Millisecond Timing Instrument

Embedded Hardware · Infrared Gates · Arduino C++

Eliminates human stopwatch latency in athletic sprint trials using paired optical infrared tripwires. Hardware interrupts track splits with millisecond resolution, rendering instantaneous race analytics to an I2C display.

SPEC: Arduino Microcontroller + IR Optocouplers + I2C Bus + Hardware Timers
FIG. 3.2 Enterprise AI · Infosys Springboard

Helpdesk.ai — Neural Support Orchestrator

DistilBERT · Cosine Deduplication · Gemini · Supabase RLS

Enterprise IT ticket routing platform. Employs fine-tuned DistilBERT to classify inbound corporate queries in <100ms, extracts NER tags, checks duplicate tickets via vector similarity, and delegates auto-resolution to Gemini.

SPEC: PyTorch + Hugging Face Spaces + Supabase RLS + Stripe Tiered Subscriptions
FIG. 3.3 Acoustic Safety · Public Health

Dhwanix — Decibel Noise Pollution Sentinel

ESP32 · Analog Preamp · Cloud Decibel Telemetry

Continuous noise monitor designed for quiet zones in hospitals and academic libraries. Tracks weighted sound levels, flags legal threshold violations with visual alarms, and syncs environmental telemetry over WiFi.

FIG. 3.4 Power Electronics · Edge ML

SHEM — Smart Home Energy Manager

ESP8266 · Non-Invasive CT Sensing · Gemini Predictive Forecasting

Precision electrical mains monitor with Gemini predictive models to catch appliance power leaks and automate load-shedding via smartphone.
