CHAPTER II INVENTIONS · APPARATUS

The Apparatus: Featured Inventions

Physical hardware artifacts and intelligent digital systems built, deployed, and tested in the field.

FIG. 2.1
★ 1st Prize Winner · ₹10,000 Award

RaceXplorer — Optical Millisecond Timing Instrument

Category: Embedded Hardware · Infrared Gates · Arduino C++

A low-cost optical race tracking instrument built to eliminate human stop-watch latency in sprint athletic trials. Utilizes paired infrared optical trip-wire sensors wired into a dedicated microcontroller interrupts loop, calculating splits with millisecond resolution and writing real-time metrics to an I2C alphanumeric display. Awarded First Prize across competitive departments at the annual College Innovation Expo.

Architecture: Arduino / Atmel · IR Optocoupler Beam · I2C Bus · Precision Clock Counter
FIG. 2.2
Enterprise AI · Infosys Springboard

Helpdesk.ai — Neural Support Orchestrator

Category: Machine Learning · DistilBERT · Gemini · Supabase RLS

A multi-tenant enterprise IT ticketing engine designed to classify, prioritize, and auto-resolve inbound corporate inquiries. Employs a fine-tuned DistilBERT classification pipeline to categorize tickets in under 100 milliseconds, performs NER metadata extraction, identifies duplicate tickets through vector cosine similarity, and delegates auto-resolution tasks to the Gemini API. Secured through a 4-tier role-based Supabase Row-Level Security architecture.

Architecture: Python / PyTorch · Hugging Face Spaces · Supabase RLS · Stripe Billing
FIG. 2.3
Acoustic Safety · Public Health

Dhwanix — Noise Pollution Sentinel

Category: Environmental IoT · Decibel Telemetry · ESP32

An IoT-enabled continuous acoustic monitoring sentinel engineered specifically for quiet zones—including intensive care hospital wings and university libraries. Captures analog sound pressure levels, computes weighted decibel metrics, triggers instant visual/auditory warnings when legal thresholds are breached, and uploads historical trends over WiFi for environmental auditing.

Architecture: ESP32 Microcontroller · Precision Microphone Preamp · Cloud Data Pipeline
FIG. 2.4
Smart Grid · Predictive AI

SHEM — Smart Home Energy Manager

Category: Power Electronics · Edge Sensing · Gemini Forecasting

An electrical mains metering and load-distribution ecosystem. Measures real-time current draws via non-invasive CT sensors, correlates usage profiles against Gemini predictive forecasting models to detect anomalous appliance power leakage, and allows homeowners to shed non-critical loads remotely through smartphone telemetry.

Architecture: ESP8266 · Current Transformer Interface · Gemini Analytics · Blynk Protocol
FIG. 2.5
Embedded Automation

Futuristic IoT Home Automation System

Category: Electrical Switching · Microcontroller Firmware · Fail-Safe Logic

A robust, multi-channel household automation terminal. Built with optical relay isolation to prevent electrical back-EMF, supporting both cloud-synchronized toggle commands and physical tactile overrides to guarantee uninterrupted local functionality during internet dropouts.

Architecture: Multi-relay bank · ESP8266 · Local State Machine · Mobile Dashboard
