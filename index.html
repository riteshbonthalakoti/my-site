The Apparatus: Hardware

Physical hardware systems and sensor devices designed, built, and validated in the field.

FIG. 3.1 ★ 1st Prize Winner · ₹10,000 Cash Award

RaceXplorer — Optical Millisecond Timing Instrument

Embedded Hardware · Infrared Beam Gates · Arduino C++

A high-precision timing instrument engineered to eliminate human stop-watch latency in athletic trials. Paired optical infrared tripwires trigger hardware interrupts to calculate split times with millisecond resolution, rendering instantaneous analytics to an I2C alphanumeric display. Won 1st Prize at the College Innovation Expo.

FIG. 3.2 Acoustic Safety · Public Health

Dhwanix — Noise Pollution Sentinel

ESP32 · Precision Microphone Preamp · WiFi Telemetry

A continuous acoustic monitor engineered for quiet zones in intensive care hospitals and academic libraries. Computes weighted decibel levels, triggers immediate visual threshold alarms, and logs historical trends over WiFi.

FIG. 3.3 Smart Grid · Predictive AI

SHEM — Smart Home Energy Manager

ESP8266 · Non-Invasive CT Sensing · Gemini Forecasting

An electrical mains monitor paired with Gemini AI predictive forecasting models to detect anomalous appliance power leakage and automate load-shedding via mobile telemetry.

The Apparatus: AI & Software

Machine learning architectures, neural triage engines, and production web applications.

FIG. 4.1 Enterprise AI · Infosys Springboard

Helpdesk.ai — Neural Support Orchestrator

DistilBERT · Cosine Deduplication · Gemini · Supabase RLS

A multi-tenant enterprise IT ticketing platform. Fine-tuned DistilBERT classifies support requests in under 100ms, extracts NER tags, checks duplicate tickets via vector cosine similarity, and delegates auto-resolution to Gemini. Secured through a 4-tier role-based Supabase Row-Level Security architecture.

FIG. 4.2 Embedded Automation · Electrical Switching

Futuristic IoT Home Automation System

ESP8266 · Optical Relay Bank · Fail-Safe Logic

A multi-channel household automation terminal. Built with optical relay isolation to prevent electrical back-EMF, supporting both cloud-synchronized toggle commands and physical tactile overrides for resilience during network dropouts.

FIG. 4.3 Mobile Application · Productivity

Android · Local SQLite · Habit Engine

A minimalist Android task management and habit building application engineered to eliminate cognitive friction, maintain consistency streaks, and encourage deep work.
